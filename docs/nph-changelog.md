- 29 Oct 2026 · v2.57 · Owner decisions built (v2.56 was a test-only build, folded in here). Lil H's Re-plan → Everything picks the least-used protein first, keeping salmon once a week, no harsh foods together and no repeats. A thaw reminder now assumes you took the food out, moves it to the fridge, and shows an "It's still frozen" button to undo that. You can only delete your own recipe notes. Checked: Lil H's times (8:30am / 11:30am / 4pm) in the Cooked date window, and no shared recipe uses P's dislikes (now an audit check).
